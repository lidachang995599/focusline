FocusLine is installed

A draggable reading line that dims everything else. Built for ADHD and dyslexia readers — no account, no tracking, nothing leaves your browser.

1

Open any long page

Click the FocusLine icon, flip the switch, and the page dims around the line you are reading.

2

Put the line where your eyes rest

Drag the handle on the right, or press Alt + ↑ / ↓. Add Shift to change the band height.

3

Make the page quiet

Calm mode stops animations. You can also hide floating banners and sidebars with one click.

Your settings follow the site

Every website remembers its own line height, dim level, and hidden elements.

Toggle anywhere Alt+Shift+F

Privacy

FocusLine stores your settings in chrome.storage.local on this device. It never sends page content, browsing history, or settings anywhere, and it makes no network requests at all.

Pin FocusLine to your toolbar for one-click access.
