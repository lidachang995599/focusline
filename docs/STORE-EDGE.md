# Microsoft Edge Add-ons listing — draft (0.2.1)

Primary launch target while the Chrome Web Store $5 fee is blocked. Upload the **same
package** as Chrome: `release/focusline-0.2.1.zip` (manifest at the zip root, MV3,
218 files, 2.1 MB).

## Why the same package works on Edge

Edge 120+ is Chromium 120+, and everything the extension uses is supported there:

- `chrome.*` namespace, MV3 service worker, `chrome.scripting`, `chrome.storage.local`
- `optional_host_permissions` (per-site prompt, no install warning)
- `chrome.commands` (`Alt+Shift+F`, `Alt+Shift+L`)
- CSS Custom Highlight API (colour guide) and `caretRangeFromPoint` (snap / Alt+click)
- `minimum_chrome_version: "120"` is a Chrome-only key; Edge ignores unknown keys.

If Partner Center ever rejects the package over `minimum_chrome_version`, delete that one
line from `dist/manifest.json`, rezip and upload again.

### Detailed description

```
Reading on a screen means reading through banners, sidebars, autoplay video and a dozen things competing for your attention. FocusLine puts one adjustable line where your eyes rest and dims everything else.

WORKS ON PDFs — UNLIKE THE USUAL READING RULERS

Chrome and Edge cannot let extensions draw over the built-in PDF viewer, which is why most reading rulers stop working the moment you open a paper. FocusLine ships its own PDF reader: open a PDF link from the toolbar, paste a link (including arXiv-style URLs that do not end in .pdf), or drop a local file into the reader. The same line, dimming, lock shortcut and Alt+click placement work there. Text stays selectable, and the reader can read the document aloud with local voices, starting from the line you are on.

WHAT IT DOES

• A draggable reading line that dims the rest of the page
• Adjustable height and dim level
• Follow-cursor mode: the line moves with your pointer
• Snap to text lines: the line parks on the exact line under your pointer and keeps following the text when you scroll
• Lock the line to the text: it scrolls with the paragraph instead of the screen
• Alt+click to drop the line on a line of text without changing your chosen height
• Alt+right-click (or Alt+Shift+L) releases the lock
• Calm mode: stops animations and transitions that pull your eyes away
• Hide distracting elements such as ads and sidebars with one click
• Adjust text size, line height and letter spacing
• Paper, dark and high-contrast reading themes
• Optional colour guide: alternating word or sentence colours (pick your own)
• Remembers a separate setup for every website
• Keyboard control: Alt+Shift+F to toggle, Alt+Shift+L to lock, Alt+↑/↓ to move, Alt+Shift+↑/↓ to resize
• Rebind the move keys to whatever fits your keyboard

WHO IT IS FOR

• Adults with ADHD who lose their place in long articles and dashboards
• Readers with dyslexia who use a reading ruler on paper
• Anyone who wants one line of calm on a noisy page

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• No account, no sign-up, no analytics
• Your settings never leave your browser
• No permission warnings at install — the extension only touches a tab after you click it
• No ads, no data selling, no third-party scripts

FocusLine is free. Every feature is included: no account, no trial, no ads, no tip jar. Questions or feature requests: lidachang78@gmail.com

NOTE

FocusLine cannot run on browser-internal pages (edge://, chrome://) or on the Edge Add-ons store itself. It also cannot draw over the built-in PDF viewer, which is why PDFs open in the built-in FocusLine Reader instead.
```

## Submission steps

1. Sign in to <https://partner.microsoft.com/dashboard/microsoftedge/> with a Microsoft account
   (registration is free, no card).
2. Create a new extension → upload `release/focusline-0.2.1.zip`.
3. Fill the listing from the table above; upload screenshots at 1280×800.
4. Paste the hosted privacy policy URL.
5. Submit. Review usually takes 1–7 days.
