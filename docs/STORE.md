## Detailed description

```
Reading on a screen means reading through banners, sidebars, autoplay video and a dozen things competing for your attention. For readers with ADHD or dyslexia, that is often the difference between finishing a page and giving up.

FocusLine puts a single adjustable line where your eyes rest, and dims everything else.

WORKS ON PDFs — UNLIKE THE USUAL READING RULERS

Chrome's built-in PDF viewer cannot be modified by extensions, which is why most reading rulers stop working the moment you open a paper. FocusLine ships its own PDF reader: open a PDF link from the toolbar, paste a link (works for arXiv-style URLs that do not end in .pdf), or drop a local file into the reader. The same line, dimming, lock shortcut and Alt+click placement work on every page. Text stays selectable and copyable, and the reader can speak the document aloud with local voices, starting from the line you are on.

WHAT IT DOES

• A draggable reading line that dims the rest of the page
• Adjustable height and dim level, positioned exactly where you read
• Follow-cursor mode: the line moves with your pointer
• Snap to text lines: the line parks on the exact line under your pointer — no dragging, no height fiddling
• Calm mode: stops animations and transitions that pull your eyes away
• Hide distracting elements with one click
• Adjust text size, line height and letter spacing
• Lock the line to the text you are reading: it scrolls with the page instead of chasing the pointer
• Paper, dark and high-contrast reading themes
• Optional colour guide: alternating word colours (pick your own) or a highlight on the current sentence (off by default)
• Remembers a separate setup for every website
• Keyboard control: Alt+Shift+F to toggle, Alt+Shift+L to lock, Alt+↑/↓ to move, Alt+Shift+↑/↓ to resize
• Alt+click anywhere to drop the line on that exact line of text — it stays where you put it
• Alt+right-click (or Alt+Shift+L) releases the lock without moving the line
• Rebind the move-up / move-down keys to whatever fits your keyboard
• Built-in PDF reader for papers, reports and textbooks
• Read aloud in the PDF reader, starting from the line you are on
• Colour guide in the reader too: alternating word or sentence colours

WHO IT IS FOR

• Adults with ADHD who lose their place in long articles and dashboards
• Readers with dyslexia who use a reading ruler on paper
• Anyone who wants one line of calm on a noisy page

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• No account, no sign-up, no analytics
• Your settings never leave your browser
• No permission warnings at install — the extension only touches a tab after you click it
• No ads, no data selling, no third-party scripts

PRICING

FocusLine is free. Every feature — colours, element hiding, per-site auto-run — is included, with no account, no trial, no ads and no tip jar. Questions or feature requests go to lidachang78@gmail.com.

NOTE

FocusLine cannot run on browser-internal pages (chrome://) or the Chrome Web Store. It also cannot draw over Chrome's built-in PDF viewer, which is why PDFs open in the built-in FocusLine Reader instead.

MISSING YOUR LANGUAGE?

FocusLine ships in 10 languages. If yours is not one of them, email lidachang78@gmail.com and it will be added as soon as possible.
```
