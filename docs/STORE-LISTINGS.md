### Detailed

```
FocusLine — your own reading ruler for the screen

Reading on a screen usually isn't quiet reading. It's reading between banners, pop-ups, autoplay video, floating sidebars and a dozen other things asking for attention. Lose focus for a second and you forget which line you were on.

FocusLine puts an adjustable reading line exactly where your eyes already rest. It keeps the line you're reading and dims the rest of the page. Nothing about your reading habit has to change, and you don't have to switch to another page: the tab you're on becomes a calmer place to read, immediately.

Works in PDFs too

FocusLine ships its own PDF reader: open a PDF link, paste one of those arXiv addresses without a file extension, or drag a local file into the window. The line, the dimming, the lock and the keyboard shortcuts work exactly as they do on the web. Text stays selectable and copyable, and you can start reading aloud from the line you're on, using local voices.

Everything you need for reading

You can tune every detail of the line to your own habit:

• a highlighted reading line you can drag, resize, and let dim the area around it
• follow-cursor mode: the line moves to wherever your pointer rests, no dragging
• snap to text: it aligns to the line under the pointer and stays with the text when you scroll
• paragraph lock: the line stays on the paragraph you're reading and scrolls with the content, not the screen
• Alt+click: pin the line to any line without touching the height you set
• Alt+right-click: release the pin whenever you want
• calm mode: stops page animations; together with “hide elements” it also clears ads and sidebars
• text size, line height and letter spacing, for articles and papers of any length
• three built-in themes — paper, dark and high contrast — so long sessions don't tire your eyes
• an optional colour guide that alternates colours by word or by sentence, in colours you choose
• per-site settings, so every site opens the way you left it
• full keyboard control: Alt+Shift+F to toggle, Alt+Shift+L to lock, Alt+↑/↓ to nudge, Alt+Shift+↑/↓ to resize; the move keys are rebindable in the popup, the toggle and lock keys in your browser's extension shortcuts page

Built for people who need to focus

FocusLine was made for these readers from the start:

• adults with ADHD who lose their line in long articles
• readers with dyslexia who have always used a physical reading ruler
• students and researchers who get through dozens of pages a day and don't want the page's noise interrupting
• anyone who just wants one quiet place to rest their eyes on a noisy web page

Privacy with no extra steps

No account, no sign-up, no collection of behaviour data. Every setting stays in your local browser and nothing is uploaded to any server. There are no unnecessary permission requests at install — the extension only takes effect on the tab you activate it on. No ads, no third-party scripts, and no user data to sell.

FocusLine is open source under the MIT licence: https://github.com/yourname/focusline

FocusLine has been completely free from day one, with every feature open: no in-app purchases, no paywall, and no plan to lock the core behind a membership later. If you run into a problem or want a new feature, leave a note on the store page or email lidachang78@gmail.com.

Open any web page or PDF now, and you have a reading line of your own, undisturbed.
```
